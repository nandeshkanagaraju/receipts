# Romanised Hindi (Hinglish) markers. One token per line; `#` starts a comment.
#
# Same principle as the Tamil file: function words and question words, never
# content words, because code-mixed Hindi keeps its business nouns in English.
#
# Short grammatical particles that collide with words in other languages are
# deliberately ABSENT -- `se`, `ka`, `ki`, `ko`, `hai`, `me` -- because two
# letters of evidence is not evidence, and a false Hindi reading of an English
# question costs more than a missed Hinglish one. CUT-LINE note: code-mixed
# Hindi is the droppable half of M8, and this file is short by intent rather
# than by neglect.
